visual-conformance gate. It was acquired read-only on 2026-08-13 from Open

## Authority and use

- The feature specification and approved reconciliation remain authoritative
  for product semantics. These files are authoritative for visual language,
  hierarchy, and composition only where they do not conflict with those
  semantics.
- Canonical desktop comparison viewport: `1440x900`, device scale factor `1`.
- Canonical page references: `preview-daily-habits-fixed.png`, the Week panel in
  `preview-orbit-v2.png` together with `weekly-dashboard-v2.html`, and
  `preview-history-polished.png`. `preview-release-polish.png` is retained as
  the approved persistence-failure treatment, not mislabeled as a Week page.
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript, tokens, brand notes, and the ORBIT system artifact are
  retained so a reviewer can inspect the visual source rather than relying on
  screenshots alone.
- The frozen files must not be silently refreshed. Any replacement requires a
  new manifest, new hashes, and explicit product-owner approval.
